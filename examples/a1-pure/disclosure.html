Get started

Disclosure

The disclosure component uses the native <details> and <summary> elements. Apply class="a1-details" to get a bordered panel with a styled toggle — no JavaScript required.

Single disclosure

A single <details class="a1-details">. The <summary> is the clickable trigger; any sibling element becomes the revealed panel content.

<details class="a1-details">
  <summary>What is A1 Pure?</summary>
  <p class="a1-p">Panel content here.</p>
</details>

Open by default

Add the open attribute to render the panel expanded on page load.

<details class="a1-details" open>
  <summary>Expanded by default</summary>
  <p class="a1-p">Panel content here.</p>
</details>

Accordion group

Stack multiple .a1-details elements directly. Adjacent panels are spaced with a small gap between them via the .a1-details + .a1-details selector.

<details class="a1-details">
  <summary>Question one</summary>
  <p class="a1-p">Answer here.</p>
</details>
<details class="a1-details">
  <summary>Question two</summary>
  <p class="a1-p">Answer here.</p>
</details>
<details class="a1-details">
  <summary>Question three</summary>
  <p class="a1-p">Answer here.</p>
</details>

Rich panel content

Panel content can include any A1-styled elements — paragraphs, lists, code, and more.

<details class="a1-details">
  <summary>Getting started checklist</summary>
  <ol class="a1-ol">
    <li>Step one.</li>
    <li>Step two.</li>
  </ol>
</details>
