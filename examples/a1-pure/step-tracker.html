Step Tracker

Use Step Tracker to show a user's position within a sequence. The current step renders as a wider dark pill; all other steps render as small neutral dots. The component is display-only — for interactive step navigation use the Tabs component with variant="progress".

Default

Apply role="img" and aria-label to describe the step position to assistive technology. All child elements are aria-hidden="true".

<div class="a1-step-tracker" role="img" aria-label="Step 1 of 5">
  <div class="a1-step-tracker__step a1-step-tracker__step--current" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <div class="a1-step-tracker__step" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <div class="a1-step-tracker__step" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <div class="a1-step-tracker__step" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <div class="a1-step-tracker__step" aria-hidden="true"></div>
</div>

Alignments

Add an alignment modifier to justify the tracker within its container. --center and --right use flexbox justification. --full expands the active pill to fill all remaining space — useful inside cards and bottom sheets.

left (default)

a1-step-tracker--center

a1-step-tracker--right

a1-step-tracker--full

<!-- center -->
<div class="a1-step-tracker a1-step-tracker--center" role="img" aria-label="Step 2 of 5"> ... </div>

<!-- right -->
<div class="a1-step-tracker a1-step-tracker--right" role="img" aria-label="Step 2 of 5"> ... </div>

<!-- full — active pill expands to fill remaining space -->
<div class="a1-step-tracker a1-step-tracker--full" role="img" aria-label="Step 2 of 5"> ... </div>
