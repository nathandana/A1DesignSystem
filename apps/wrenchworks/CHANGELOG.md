# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — July 30, 2026

- **First playable release** — Added a 10-business mechanic empire, automated
  idle income, local saves, offline progress, strategic decisions, milestones
  and repeatable franchises.
- **Original garage world** — Added responsive code-native cars, lifts, shops,
  mechanics and city scenery with reduced-motion support.
- **No-ads economy** — Added a complete long-running progression model with no
  advertising, purchases, analytics or account requirement.
