# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — July 30, 2026

- **Walkable workshop** — Replaced the control-dashboard loop with direct
  character movement, touch joystick, tap-to-walk, keyboard controls,
  proximity stations, and multi-stop service routes.
- **Illustrated game world** — Replaced primitive CSS scenery with three
  production illustrated workshop tiers and a detailed mechanic character.
- **In-world interface** — Moved upgrades, staffing, goals, city travel, and
  settings into panels reached from physical shop locations.
- **Feedback hierarchy** — Uses full-screen overlays for disruptive offline,
  franchise, and reset moments, while routine shop messages auto-dismiss.

## 1.0.0 — July 30, 2026

- **First playable release** — Added a 10-business mechanic empire, automated
  idle income, local saves, offline progress, strategic decisions, milestones
  and repeatable franchises.
- **Original garage world** — Added responsive code-native cars, lifts, shops,
  mechanics and city scenery with reduced-motion support.
- **No-ads economy** — Added a complete long-running progression model with no
  advertising, purchases, analytics or account requirement.
